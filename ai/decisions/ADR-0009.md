# ADR-0009: Two Page Purposes, Gated Differently

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-09-14 |

## Decision

A page declares one of two purposes, and the existence gate applies the test that fits it.

- **`differentiated_informational`** — must hold an approved angle and establish information gain. Henderson.
- **`service_area_transactional`** — must name a configured service area and rest on publishable business-provided claims. It does **not** need information gain, because that is not what it is for.

Both remain subject to the structural, entity and FAQ differentiation checks. Only the information-gain requirement is conditional.

## Context

Phase 3B measured a templated corpus and the engine was built to stop it recurring. The control it settled on — no page without an approved angle carrying information gain — was calibrated against informational pages, and it works: Henderson earned its page on municipal-code evidence, and eight pages were correctly refused.

Applying that same test to a service-area page asks the wrong question. Someone searching "junk car buyers North Las Vegas" is not looking to learn something. They want to know whether the business covers their address, what happens next, and how to start. A page serving that intent succeeds by being clear and accurate, not by teaching a reader something its siblings do not.

Judged on information gain such a page can only fail, which left the engine unable to express an ordinary and legitimate page type. The gap showed as a false negative: North Las Vegas is a configured service area the business genuinely serves, and the only available verdict was "no supported angle".

There was a second, quieter error behind it. The Henderson pilot shipped with no service claim in prose — no mention of towing, payment or timing — because no claim id supported them. That was recorded at the time as the system working. It was half right: the constraint was correct, but the claims were missing. Free towing, same-day pickup, no hidden fees and the three-step process are statements the business makes about itself, and `business-config.php` is an authoritative source for exactly that. They belong in the ledger, and their absence was an omission rather than a standard.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Drop the angle requirement for location pages** — Rejected. It is the control that produced the eight correct refusals, and removing it re-opens the door the whole engine was built to close.
- **Invent an angle for North Las Vegas** — Rejected, and specifically refused earlier in this work. The city's own provisions could not be retrieved, and the one rule found (NRS 487.290) is statewide. Cloning Henderson's angle with a different city name is the failure this system exists to prevent.
- **Let service-area pages skip differentiation entirely** — Rejected. That is how twelve near-identical pages were produced in the first place. Structural, entity and FAQ checks still apply, so a service-area page that copies a sibling's shape is still blocked.
- **Put business claims in each page's ledger** — Rejected. Nine claims duplicated across a dozen files drift the moment a service promise changes. They live once in `claims/_shared-business.json` and are merged into every brief.

## Consequences

- `page_purpose` is required on every production brief, and the gate branches on it.
- `service_area_transactional` requires: the slug is a configured, non-primary service area; at least one publishable business claim; and the same structural differentiation every page faces. No angle, no information gain.
- Business claims carry `claim_boundary` where the config is narrower than the copy would like. "Same-day pickup available" may not become "guaranteed". "We can still help without a title" may not become a legal statement about title — that needs a DMV or statutory source, and its absence is what put a false claim on the Henderson page once already. The firm-offer claim supports no dollar figure.
- The eight `NO_SUPPORTED_DIFFERENTIATION` pages are **not** unblocked wholesale. Each must still be reclassified deliberately, and each still has to pass structural differentiation against its siblings. The recommendation to consolidate the unincorporated-county pages stands on its own merits and is unaffected.
- Henderson keeps `differentiated_informational`. Its evidence is stronger than a service-area page needs, and downgrading it would discard the angle it earned.

Contract: `production-brief.schema.json`. Gate: `content-engine/lib/briefing.py`. Shared claims: `content-engine/claims/_shared-business.json`.
