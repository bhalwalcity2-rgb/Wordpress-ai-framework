# ADR-0006: Evidence Gating for Generated Content

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-09-03 |

## Decision

No content may state as fact anything the research artifacts do not support. Every externally verifiable statement lives in a claim ledger with typed provenance; every page must hold a distinct, evidence-backed angle before it is written; and a page whose research yields no supported angle returns `NO_SUPPORTED_DIFFERENTIATION` rather than an invented one.

## Context

Phase 3B established that this corpus is templated — 50 of 68 sibling pairs share an identical structure, six pages differ from their siblings only by place name. The obvious next step is a writer that produces better copy. Building it first would be a mistake.

The corpus already demonstrates why. Live pages assert that Nevada statute lets an HOA coordinate towing, and that a duplicate title costs $20 via DMV Form VP-012 for vehicles model year 2010 or older. Those may well be true. Nothing in the repository shows where they came from, and a writer with no evidence contract will reproduce them, or invent their equivalents, with the same confidence.

There is a second failure the same contract prevents. Asked to differentiate twelve pages, a generator will always produce twelve differentiations — a landmark here, a ZIP there — because producing nothing is not a behaviour it falls into. Phase 3B named that pattern precisely: swapping *Maryland Parkway* for *Boulder Highway* is a new string, not new information. Unless "no supported angle" is a first-class, expected output, the system manufactures thin pages and reports success.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Let the writer research inline as it drafts** — Rejected. Evidence and prose would be produced by the same pass, so a claim's support could never be audited separately from the sentence that used it, and nothing would persist between runs.
- **Require sources only for legal and regulatory claims** — Rejected as insufficient. Pricing, guarantees, and process timings are the ones a customer acts on. They carry a lower risk tier, not none.
- **Score uniqueness after generation and regenerate on failure** — Rejected. Optimises against the detector. A generator told only that its output is too similar will rephrase until the score passes, which is the superficial differentiation the whole phase exists to prevent. The angle must be fixed *before* drafting.
- **Have the assistant fill the dossiers from its own knowledge** — Rejected, and the reason is the load-bearing one: a model-generated statement is not evidence. Recording one as a sourced fact would launder a guess into a citation, which is worse than an empty dossier, because an empty dossier is visibly incomplete.

## Consequences

- Six artifact types per page, each schema-validated: `place/`, `claims/`, `angles/`, `intent/`, `entities/`, `queries/`.
- Provenance is explicit. Only `verified_fact`, `sourced_fact`, and `business_provided` may be stated as fact. `hypothesis`, `editorial_opportunity`, and `unknown_needs_verification` may not, at any confidence.
- `claim_type: government_legal_regulatory` requires `official_government` or `primary_source` plus a `verified_on` date before `publishable` can be true. Reputable secondary reporting is not sufficient for a statute or a fee.
- Angle ids are globally unique, and an approved or used angle belongs to exactly one page. Reassignment requires a documented override.
- An angle whose statement collapses when place names are masked is rejected as superficial, whatever its status.
- Being live on the site confers nothing. Every claim scaffolded from existing copy is recorded `source_type: unverified`, `publishable: false` — 39 of them on Henderson alone.
- `NO_SUPPORTED_DIFFERENTIATION` is a valid terminal outcome carrying a required recommendation: merge, repurpose, broaden, or exclude. Eight of twelve pages currently return it. Fewer useful pages beat more thin ones.
- Bootstrapping performs no research. It produces a work order — open questions someone must answer — not findings.
