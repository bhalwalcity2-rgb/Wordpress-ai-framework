# ADR-0007: The Brief Is the Writer's Only Input

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-09-05 |

## Decision

A page may only be written from a production brief that passed the page-existence gate, and a brief may only be produced for a page holding an **approved** angle with at least one publishable claim. The writer refuses otherwise. Drafts are written exclusively to `content-engine/drafts/`; nothing in this stage may write into a theme's `content/` directory.

## Context

Phases 3B and 3C established what goes wrong when a writer is under-constrained. The corpus was templated in a way that word-level checks could not see — 50 of 68 sibling pairs shared an identical section plan while their wording differed enough to look original. And its most confident claims were the least supported: a live page asserted that a Nevada HOA can tow a car from an owner's driveway, which the statute contradicts.

Both failures share a cause. A writer given a keyword, a city and a page title has nothing to work from except the sibling pages and its own fluency, so it reproduces their structure and invents the facts it needs. Neither failure is fixed by asking for better output; both are fixed by changing what the writer is allowed to receive.

There is a second reason the gate must be code rather than instruction. Asked to differentiate twelve pages, a generator will produce twelve differentiations — a landmark here, a ZIP there — because returning nothing is not a behaviour it falls into. Refusal has to be structural, and it has to happen before any prose exists.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Generate first, score afterwards, regenerate on failure** — Rejected. It optimises against the detector. Told only that its output is too similar, a generator rewords until the score passes, which is exactly the superficial differentiation Phase 3B measured. Sibling constraints must reach the writer *before* it writes, not after.
- **Let the writer read sibling pages for context** — Rejected. A sibling is the single most dangerous input available: the fastest route to a passable page is to paraphrase one. Siblings enter the brief only as constraints — structures to avoid, questions already answered — never as source text.
- **A standard section template for location pages** — Rejected, and this is the specific thing the corpus proves wrong. Identical structure was the strongest templating signal measured. Structure is derived per page from its purpose and evidence; the brief supplies working notes, not headings.
- **Let the writer state well-known general facts without a claim id** — Rejected. "Well known" is indistinguishable from "confidently wrong" at the point of writing, which is how the HOA towing claim reached production. The allowed claim list is the entire factual budget.
- **Write drafts straight into the theme's `content/`** — Rejected. A draft that lands where the renderer reads is published, whatever it is called. The drafts directory is what makes review possible.

## Consequences

- Four stages, following the existing `content-engine/bin/` convention: `create-content-brief.py`, `validate-content-brief.py`, `write-content.py`, `validate-draft.py`.
- The gate runs at brief time **and again** at write time. A brief is a file, and a file can be edited; the writer re-verifies the angle against the registry rather than trusting what it is handed.
- Only `approved` and `used` angles authorise writing. `proposed`, `researched`, `evidence_backed`, `unsupported` and `retired` do not, at any confidence.
- Blocked briefs are still written, recording why the page may not proceed and what to do instead — merge, broaden, repurpose, or exclude. A refusal with a reason is more useful than a missing file.
- The repository takes on no AI API (`AI_RULES.md` §4). `write-content.py` is the control layer, not the author: it assembles the authoring package and validates the result. Constraints live in code that cannot be talked out of them; prose is written by something that can read.
- **Current state: 0 approved angles, so all 12 pages are refused.** That is the system working, and `selftest-pipeline.py` asserts both halves — that the real corpus is refused, and that a fixture with genuine evidence passes. The fixture never survives the run.
- Phase 3B thresholds travel in the brief as calibrated editorial signals, not blind universal rules, and are re-measurable after any rewrite.

Contracts: `production-brief.schema.json`, `draft.schema.json`. Gate: `content-engine/lib/briefing.py`. Detail: `docs/architecture/content-production.md`.
